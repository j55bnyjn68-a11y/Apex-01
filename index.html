3-Axis vs. APEX-01 5-Axis

Conventional planar slicing vs. multi-planar, curved deposition.

Conventional 3-Axis (Planar)

  • Flat horizontal 2.5D layers → severe "staircase" surface roughness
  • Overhangs >45° need sacrificial supports — up to 30% material waste
  • Inter-layer Z-bonds 30–50% weaker than in-plane strength → delamination

APEX-01 5-Axis (Multi-Planar)

  • Dynamic nozzle orientation prints onto curved & inclined substrates
  • 0% temporary supports — saves up to 81% raw filament
  • Isotropic strength: fill follows stress vectors → up to 6.35x tensile
  • 51% less print time · 63% less surface roughness (Ra)

Kinematic Optimization & Build Envelope

Why 5 DOF, and the geometry behind the 46 cm reach.

5 DOF vs. 6 DOF

A circular extrusion nozzle is axisymmetric — rotating it about its own vertical axis (the 6th joint) changes nothing in deposition. Removing that joint cuts motor weight and wrist backlash, letting analytical Inverse Kinematics run in real time on affordable embedded microcontrollers.
